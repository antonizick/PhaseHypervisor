# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is published at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, Nick Antonizick, at nick@antonizick.com. Reports are handled privately.
